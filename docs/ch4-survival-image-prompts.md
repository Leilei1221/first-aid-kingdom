## 2. 氣象預報圖（#1）
中文說明：像氣象署預報圖的「簡化遊戲版」。畫面分成左中右三欄，代表「今天、明天、後天」。每一欄上方是天空的圖示（太陽、雲、雪花、強風線條），中間是一座山的剪影，下方一個風向箭頭（大小代表風的強弱）和一根溫度計（紅色高度代表氣溫，**不要數字**）。第一欄晴朗、風小；第二欄大雪、風大；第三欄多雲、風中。背景是淺色的紙質地圖質感，帶一點手繪風格。**沒有任何文字或數字**，日期與說明我用程式疊上去。
```
A hand-painted fantasy RPG weather-forecast chart on warm parchment paper, divided into three equal vertical panels side by side (today, tomorrow, the day after). Each panel has, from top to bottom: a large sky icon (panel 1 a bright sun, panel 2 heavy snowflakes with strong wind swirls, panel 3 thick clouds), a small mountain silhouette in the middle, a big arrow showing wind direction (thin for light wind in panel 1, thick for strong wind in panel 2, medium in panel 3), and a simple thermometer at the bottom with a colored bar showing the temperature (high in panel 1, very low in panel 2, medium in panel 3). Clean, clear, easy to read at a glance, painterly texture, wide 16:9 composition. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark. No red cross anywhere.
```
再生一張變體（風小晴天／全是暴風雪／混合）更好，我之後可以做不同的三天組合。
